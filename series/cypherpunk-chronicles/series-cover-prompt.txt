In the background, VOID (The Digital Destroyer) looms as a corrupted figure, his BLACK hair appearing to be made of digital static, wearing his BLACK and DARK RED dark costume, his BRIGHT RED eyes glowing with destructive energy as he creates digital corruption and data destruction effects.

Behind them, CORRUPTOR (The Mind Controller) appears as a corpoarate villain, his appearance shifting as he controls minds and corrupts technology, surrounded by corruption effects and mind control energy.
