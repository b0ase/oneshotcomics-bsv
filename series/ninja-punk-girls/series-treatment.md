# NINJA PUNK GIRLS
**File:** `series/ninja-punk-girls/series-treatment.md`  
**Theme:** *"Tradition meets rebellion in the shadows."*

## Core Concept
A modern take on ninja mythology, combining traditional Japanese martial arts with contemporary punk culture. The series explores themes of identity, tradition, and rebellion through the lens of female ninja warriors in a modern urban setting.

## Tone & Atmosphere
- **Primary:** Action-packed, rebellious, empowering
- **Secondary:** Cultural fusion, coming-of-age, urban fantasy
- **Visual Style:** Modern urban settings, traditional Japanese elements, punk aesthetics

## Central Themes
- **Tradition vs. Innovation:** Honoring the past while embracing the future
- **Female Empowerment:** Breaking gender stereotypes in martial arts
- **Cultural Identity:** Finding your place between different worlds
- **Rebellion & Responsibility:** When to break rules and when to follow them

## Character Dynamics
- **Kunoichi:** The traditionalist learning to adapt to modern times
- **Shadow Blade:** The stealth master, operating in the shadows
- **Neon Fist:** The energy warrior, combining old and new techniques

## Story Guidelines
- **Plot Structure:** Martial arts training, urban adventures, cultural conflicts
- **Conflict Types:** Street-level crime, cultural misunderstandings, personal growth
- **Resolution Style:** Often empowering, with characters finding their strength
- **World Building:** Modern cities with hidden ninja clans, traditional dojos

## Visual & Narrative Elements
- Modern urban environments with traditional Japanese elements
- Martial arts training and combat sequences
- Punk culture and rebellious attitudes
- Hidden ninja clans and secret societies
- Cultural fusion and identity exploration

## Story Generation Focus
- **Story 1:** Introduction to ninja traditions and character training
- **Story 2:** Escalation of urban conflicts and cultural challenges
- **Story 3:** Ultimate test of ninja skills and personal growth
