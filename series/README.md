## Content Guidelines

### Series Treatment Files
Each series has a `series-treatment.md` file containing:
- Core concept and theme
- Tone and atmosphere guidelines
- Central themes to explore
- Character dynamics and relationships
- Story guidelines and world-building elements
- Visual and narrative elements

### Story Development
When creating content for story directories:
1. **Follow the series treatment** for thematic consistency
2. **Maintain character consistency** with established traits and powers
3. **Explore central themes** of the series
4. **Use appropriate visual elements** and narrative styles
5. **Ensure quality and engagement** for readers

### Cross-Series Integration
- Characters can appear in crossovers between series
- Maintain consistency in character appearance and abilities
- Respect the unique tone and themes of each series
- Create meaningful connections that enhance the overall universe

## File Organization

### Story Files
- Use descriptive filenames for story content
- Include character names and story elements in filenames
- Maintain consistent formatting across all series
- Include metadata for story tracking and organization

### Character Integration
- Reference character image prompts from `character-images/` directory
- Use established character traits and powers consistently
- Maintain visual consistency with character designs
- Ensure character development aligns with series themes
