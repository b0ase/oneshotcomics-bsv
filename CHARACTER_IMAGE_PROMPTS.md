## QUANTUM PARADOX

### NOVA - The Time Weaver
**Series:** Quantum Paradox  
**Type:** Hero  
**Alignment:** Hero  

**IMAGE PROMPT:**
A young woman in her early 20s with very specific physical features: DARK BROWN hair (not blonde, not red, not black) that is long and flowing down to her waist, with natural waves and a slight curl at the ends. Her skin is LIGHT OLIVE TONE with a warm undertone, covered in small star-shaped patterns that glow with soft blue cosmic energy. Her eyes are LARGE AND ALMOND-SHAPED, colored DEEP SAPPHIRE BLUE with golden flecks, and they glow with temporal energy. Her face is OVAL-SHAPED with HIGH CHEEKBONES, a SMALL BUTTON NOSE, and FULL LIPS. She has a SLIM BUT ATHLETIC BUILD, standing at 5'7" with LONG LIMBS. She wears a sleek, form-fitting cosmic-themed suit that is DARK NAVY BLUE with SILVER accents, featuring quantum particle patterns, time symbols, and flowing energy trails. The suit has a HIGH COLLAR and LONG SLEEVES with GLOVES. 

**KEY VISUAL ELEMENTS:**
- DARK BROWN hair (long, wavy, waist-length)
- LIGHT OLIVE skin with star patterns
- DEEP SAPPHIRE BLUE eyes with golden flecks
- OVAL face with high cheekbones
- SLIM ATHLETIC build, 5'7"
- DARK NAVY BLUE suit with silver accents
- Quantum particle effects and time symbols
- Temporal energy glow around hands and eyes

**ART STYLE:** Modern comic book, highly detailed, dynamic lighting, epic sci-fi atmosphere

**CRITICAL CONSISTENCY NOTES:**
- Hair must ALWAYS be DARK BROWN, never blonde, red, or black
- Skin must be LIGHT OLIVE TONE, not pale white or dark
- Eyes must be DEEP SAPPHIRE BLUE with golden flecks
- Face shape must be OVAL with high cheekbones
- Build must be SLIM BUT ATHLETIC, not muscular or curvy
- Suit must be DARK NAVY BLUE with silver accents
